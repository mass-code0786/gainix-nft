# Gainix NFT Style Guide

## Core Direction
Gainix NFT should read as a luxury Web3 collection built in a black-room studio, not a playful cartoon set. Every asset should feel polished, strategic, collectible, and slightly futuristic.

## Visual Language
- Format: square portrait, centered bust or upper torso, consistent crop and head scale
- Medium: high-polish stylized illustration with premium 3D finish
- Detail level: sharp material definition, clean silhouette, controlled glow, no noisy rendering
- Tone: dark luxury, crimson-black atmosphere, restrained cyber influence
- Finish: collector-grade, auction-house polish, premium marketplace thumbnail readability

## Palette
- Base palette: obsidian black, graphite, gunmetal, smoked silver
- Signature accent: deep crimson, ember red, ruby glow
- Secondary accents: antique gold, restrained teal, low-volume ivory highlights
- Rule: red is an accent, not a flood fill

## Lighting
- Primary setup: soft frontal key with strong crimson rim light
- Secondary setup: subtle overhead specular on metal, glass, and feathers/fur
- Background mood: dark gradient, halo, or atmospheric luxury environment
- Avoid: flat daylight, pastel fills, comic-book cel shading

## Materials
- Fur and feathers: groomed, dimensional, slightly glossy, never fluffy-cartoon
- Metals: brushed gold, black chrome, gunmetal, ruby-coated alloys
- Fabrics: velvet, satin, technical nylon, matte leather, carbon weave
- Glass: smoked lenses, holographic surfaces, crisp reflections

## Anatomy and Pose
- Pose: upright, composed, premium character portrait
- Expression: controlled confidence, not slapstick exaggeration
- Readability: recognizable family silhouette even in small thumbnails
- Consistency: identical perspective baseline across all families and trait layers

## Composition Rules
- Character centered on a fixed rig
- Shoulders stay within the same frame zone across the collection
- Accessories must align to the same anchor points
- Effects should support the portrait, not cover the face

## Premium Constraints
- No childish proportions
- No random streetwear clutter
- No mismatched brush styles across categories
- No exaggerated meme faces
- No washed-out backgrounds or bright candy colors

## Asset QA Checklist
- Silhouette reads instantly
- Eyes and mouth stay aligned to the master rig
- Trait edge cleanup is production-grade
- Transparent PNG layers have no halos
- Red-black luxury language remains intact even with rare variants
